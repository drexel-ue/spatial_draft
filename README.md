## 🌟 Pedagogical & Engineering Overview

**SpatialDraft** fulfills the pedagogical vision of fusing infinite spatial canvas concepts (*Mental Canvas*, *Endless Paper*) with deliberate, engineering-grade draftsmanship (*Scott Robertson*, *DrawABox*, *Andrew Loomis*). Built from the ground up as a native **Landscape iPad** studio, every stroke is treated as a continuous physical trajectory governed by biomechanics:

- **🎯 The Ghosting & Velocity Engine**: Replaces subjective line grading with physical kinematics. By measuring instantaneous velocity derivatives ($\frac{d^2s}{dt^2}$), the engine isolates shoulder-driven ballistic strokes from wrist-driven hesitation wobbles, mapping real-time green/amber/red velocity heatmaps along your ink.
- **📐 Perspective Ellipse & Minor Axis Rule**: Solves for true perspective circles projected into 3D space using direct algebraic least-squares fitting. Enforces Scott Robertson's fundamental draftsmanship law: *the minor axis of an ellipse must be collinear with the rotational axle of the cylindrical form*.
- **📦 Isometric Carve-Outs & Voxel Subtraction**: Procedural bounding blocks (billets) ready for subtractive manufacturing and spatial visualization. Teaches visual hierarchy with distinct line weights: **Silhouette** ($4.0\text{pt}$), **Crease** ($2.5\text{pt}$), and **Hidden/Dashed** ($1.5\text{pt}$).
- **👤 3D Procedural Loomis Head Construction**: Full 3D orbital cranium featuring interactive pitch ($\pm 45^\circ$) and yaw ($\pm 60^\circ$) rotations. Traces the cranial sphere, flattened temporal cut ellipses, and proportional jaw wedge planes.
- **🏃 Kinematic Mannequin & Dynamic Pose Gestures**: Procedurally generated contrapposto human poses with flowing Lines of Action, tilted shoulder/pelvis axes, and volumetric cylindrical limb guides.
- **🔄 Closed-Loop Interconnected Skill Profile**: Performance data from precision motor drills feeds directly into the procedural generation engines of subsequent drills. If your 16-sector polar profiler detects high jitter at $135^\circ$, the system automatically tilts future ghosting targets, ellipse planes, and cranial angles to systematically reinforce weak neuromuscular pathways.
- **💡 Non-Intrusive Pedagogical Scaffolding**: Floating, dismissible coachmark tooltips provide contextual physical guidance on first launch without breaking creative flow. Persistent concept guides (`ℹ️ Guide`) offer deep biomechanical primers on shoulder mechanics, foreshortening, and line hierarchy.
- **🎨 3 High-Contrast Studio Themes**:
  - **Drafting Blueprint**: Deep cyanotype navy background (`#0B2046`) with luminous chalk-white ink and engineering gridlines.
  - **Dark Obsidian**: Matte charcoal background (`#0F1115`) with high-contrast radiant white ink.
  - **Light Studio Paper**: Clean architectural vellum (`#F8F9FB`) with crisp graphite ink.

## 🔬 Kinematic Math & Scoring Architecture

### 1. Tangential Velocity & Acceleration Derivative ($d^2s/dt^2$)

Traditional drawing apps evaluate lines purely on geometric distance ($L_2$ error to a straight line), penalizing slow, cautious lines less than fast, confident lines. In spatial draftsmanship, **confidence and velocity consistency are paramount**:

Given a discrete sequence of timestamped stroke points $P_i = (x_i, y_i, t_i)$:

$$\Delta s_i = \sqrt{(x_{i} - x_{i-1})^2 + (y_{i} - y_{i-1})^2}, \quad \Delta t_i = t_i - t_{i-1}$$

$$v_i = \frac{\Delta s_i}{\Delta t_i}, \quad a_i = \frac{v_i - v_{i-1}}{\Delta t_i}$$

The **Jitter Index** penalizes high-frequency acceleration spikes caused by wrist tremors and hesitation:

$$J = \frac{1}{N-2} \sum_{i=2}^{N-1} \left| a_i - a_{i-1} \right|$$

- $J < 0.20$: **Low Jitter** (Shoulder ball-and-socket execution)
- $0.20 \le J \le 0.40$: **Moderate Jitter** (Partial elbow compensation)
- $J > 0.40$: **High Jitter** (Hesitant wrist micro-steering)

The stroke is dynamically segmented and colored with a gradient:
- **Neon Green (`#22C55E`)**: Stable, confident cruising velocity.
- **Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Minor deceleration or entry/exit transition.
- **Coral Red (`#EF4444`)**: Abrupt hesitation or direction hesitation.

---

### 2. Fitzgibbon Algebraic Ellipse Fitting & Minor Axis Rule

To verify true perspective ellipses without expensive iterative non-linear optimization, SpatialDraft implements the **Fitzgibbon Direct Least Squares Ellipse Fitting** algorithm. An ellipse in general conic form satisfies:

$$F(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{a} = ax^2 + bxy + cy^2 + dx + ey + f = 0$$

Subject to the ellipse constraint:

$$b^2 - 4ac < 0 \implies \mathbf{a}^T \mathbf{C} \mathbf{a} = 1$$

This reduces to a generalized eigenvalue problem:

$$\mathbf{D}^T \mathbf{D} \mathbf{a} = \lambda \mathbf{C} \mathbf{a}$$

From the fitted conic coefficients $(a, b, c, d, e, f)$, the engine extracts:
- **Center**: $(x_c, y_c)$
- **Major Axis Semi-Diameter**: $a_{\text{semi}}$
- **Minor Axis Semi-Diameter**: $b_{\text{semi}}$
- **Orientation Angle**: $\theta = \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{atan2}(b, a - c)$

The **Minor Axis Error** is calculated against the expected 3D normal axle $\theta_{\text{axle}}$:

$$\Delta \theta_{\text{minor}} = \min(|\theta_{\text{minor}} - \theta_{\text{axle}}|, 180^\circ - |\theta_{\text{minor}} - \theta_{\text{axle}}|)$$

---

### 3. 16-Sector Polar Profiler & Closed-Loop Adaptive Target Selection

The human arm draws at different degrees of natural freedom depending on the angle relative to the torso. SpatialDraft discretizes the 2D plane into **16 radial angular sectors** ($22.5^\circ$ bins):

$$\text{Sector}(P) = \left\lfloor \frac{(\operatorname{atan2}(\Delta y, \Delta x) \pmod{2\pi})}{2\pi / 16} \right\rfloor$$

Each sector accumulates moving-window accuracy, velocity consistency, and jitter metrics. To close the training loop, future drill generation angles $\theta_{\text{target}}$ are selected inversely proportional to sector proficiency:

$$w_k = 1.0 - \left( \alpha \cdot \text{Accuracy}_k + \beta \cdot \text{Consistency}_k - \gamma \cdot \text{Jitter}_k \right)$$

$$\theta_{\text{target}} = \operatorname{SampleSector}(P(k) \propto w_k) \times 22.5^\circ$$

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- **Flutter SDK**: `^3.13.0`
- **Xcode**: 15.0+ with iPadOS 17+ Simulator
- **Device Support**: iPad (all sizes, optimized for landscape), macOS desktop, and Web.

### Installation

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/LamontLabs/drawing_practice.git
cd drawing_practice

# Install dependencies
flutter pub get

# Run on macOS Desktop
flutter run -d macos

# Run on Booted iPad Simulator (Landscape)
flutter run -d "iPad mini (A17 Pro)"
```
